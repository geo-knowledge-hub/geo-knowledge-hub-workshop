# GEO Knowledge Hub @ NEOSS Communities of Practice Workshop 2026

The GEO Knowledge Hub (GKH) is a central cloud-based digital library providing access to Earth Observations applications developed by GEO Work Programme Activities. It includes applications that help users working on agriculture, disaster risk reduction, urban planning, and more.

The GEO Knowledge Hub team was invited to deliver the **SA-GEO Knowledge Hub Training**, the capacity-building day of the **2026 NEOSS Communities of Practice (CoP) Integrated Workshop and Capacity-Building Training**, hosted by the National Earth Observations and Space Secretariat (NEOSS) with the Council for Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR) and the Department of Science, Technology and Innovation (DSTI), on 27–28 July 2026 at the CSIR International Convention Centre in Tshwane, South Africa.

The training runs on Day 1 (27 July) and introduces the South Africa GEO Knowledge Hub, the GEO Knowledge Hub and the Marketplace, with hands-on sessions on creating and publishing Knowledge Packages. Our goal is to show what the platform makes possible, invite the South African community to contribute as knowledge providers, and hear the feedback that will guide the next steps of the tool.

Full description, agenda and step-by-step exercises: <https://gkhub.earthobservations.org/doc/workshops/sa-geo-training-2026/>
